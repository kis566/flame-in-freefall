Flame in Freefall

Data-Powered Fire Safety Insights for the Artemis Era

In microgravity there's no buoyancy — flames are spherical and blue, not teardrop-shaped. Traditional detection fails. NASA's PSI database holds 24 combustion experiments — 12 terabytes of data — but no mission engineer has time to read them all. We turn that data into a decision tool for lunar habitats running 30%+ O₂ at ≤10.2 psi — over 40% more flammable than Earth.
